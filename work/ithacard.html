04 / App concept

IthaCard

One app that combines discovery, discounts, and loyalty, so students and locals actually meet.

Role
Research and ideation
Context
AEM 3110, with Maximilian, Jennifer, Ella, Sujan, and Francesco
Output
App concept and Google Maps prototype
Skills
Interviews, synthesis, concept design
The brief

How might we build relationships between students, residents, and visitors in Ithaca?

The design had to:

  • Appeal to and be accessible to all three groups.
  • Clearly show what's on offer.
  • Highlight the variety of local events.
  • Reward loyalty.
  • Include regular social events with small businesses.
What I did
01

Interviewed local small businesses

I interviewed Ithaca small businesses, including Botanist Café, as part of more than ten interviews across students, residents, and owners. The business side revealed what students alone couldn't.

  • A real divide: staff described a gap between students and locals.
  • Owners want connection: family-friendly spaces, game nights, and experiences that bring people in person.
  • Low-tech habits: many rely on community boards and newsletters, so any solution had to be easy to join.

Those insights shaped the business side of IthaCard: events, partnerships, and a no-cost trial.

02

Found the key themes

  • Weak ties: 83% felt disconnected from Ithaca beyond campus.
  • Awareness gaps: students stick to Collegetown, so the Commons is under-visited.
  • Transportation: TCAT is slow, so having a car decides where people can go.
  • Scattered events: info is spread out and hard to find.
03

Shaped the idea

  • The idea: an app connecting students with local businesses through personalized rewards and deals. Students save, businesses gain loyal customers.
  • User pains: new apps are hard to learn, and there's no easy way to get to the Commons.
  • Key features: events, activities, a rewards map, and a social tab.
  • Prototype: built on Google Maps, tested with three to five businesses across food, retail, and services.
  • Risks: businesses say no, people off social media get missed, or users don't see the value.
  • Value: more foot traffic and exposure for local businesses.
The solution

IthaCard: one app that combines discovery, discounts, and loyalty, so students and locals actually meet.

  • Rewards tiers: Bronze, Silver, and Gold unlock bigger discounts and exclusive events.
  • Transportation built in: live TCAT feed, walk times, and ridesharing. “Get there faster in one tap.”
  • Business-friendly: a free four-week trial, an analytics dashboard, and user reviews.
  • Community events: trivia nights, game nights, and themed weeks with local partners.

Why it works: Google, Yelp, and Facebook just list places. IthaCard gives people a reason to go and rewards them for coming back.
