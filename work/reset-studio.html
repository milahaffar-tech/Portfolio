02 / Service design

ReSET Studio

A restorative stay at the Statler Hotel that brings Ithaca's nature inside.

Role
Research and experience design
Context
DEA 1110, with Sophia Park, Josephine Kelly, Ethan Gu, and Michael Han
Output
Room redesign and amenity kit
Skills
Mind mapping, personas, journey mapping
The brief

Revitalize the guest experience at the Statler Hotel on Cornell's campus.

What I did
01

Mapping Ithaca

We started with a team mind map of everything connected to Ithaca and the Statler: academics, alumni, business, nature, and more. No filtering, just getting ideas down.

02

Finding the common thread

Looking across the branches, one theme kept showing up: nature. It connected to almost everything.

  • Alumni remember the gorges, the lake, and the seasons.
  • Academics tie into Cornell's roots in agriculture and the outdoors.
  • Business and events bring stress that nature can help relieve.
03

Turning the theme into a purpose

Guests come to the Statler between busy travel and busy campus events: graduations, reunions, talks. What they need most is rest. Ithaca's nature (gorges, stone, wood, water) is already calming, so it became the tool for a restorative stay.

Mission: turn the Statler's guest experience into a restorative one that pays homage to the natural, elegant beauty of Ithaca.

04

Understanding the guests

Three personas grounded the design.

  • Visiting parents: proud and excited, but overwhelmed by a big campus.
  • Invited speakers: nervous, tight schedules, need to feel rested.
  • Returning alumni: nostalgic, want to reconnect with Cornell.
05

Mapping the stay

A journey map followed guests from entering the room to settling in. It showed where they feel drained or overwhelmed, and which touchpoints could calm them.

The solution

A room redesign that brings Ithaca’s nature inside.

  • Earthy palette: slate stone walls and walnut beams replace busy colors.
  • Plain tan carpet: less visual noise, more calm.
  • Nature-inspired bathroom: walnut vanity and concrete sink for a spa-like feel.
  • ReSET Amenity Kit: Revitalize, Recover, Renew, Refresh. A small piece of Ithaca guests can take home.
