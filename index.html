I'm Mila, a Cornell student studying Cognitive Science and Design & Environmental Analysis. My work sits where consumer psychology, strategy, and design overlap. I think strong experiences are curated, not assembled, so I start with research into how people actually think and feel, and let that shape every decision after it.
